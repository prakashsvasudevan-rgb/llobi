About Me & LLOBI.com

Hi! I'm Pranav, a 10-year-old student in Primary 5 at West Grove Primary School, Jurong West, Singapore. I built LLOBI.com — a free games website for kids — all by myself as a mid-year project.

It all started when I wanted to learn how to build things on the internet. I picked up coding using AI tools like Claude Code, ChatGPT, and Antigravity, learning step by step as I created each new game. What began as a small experiment turned into a website with over a dozen original games, all designed and built by me.

I'm passionate about programming and technology, and I believe AI is the future of building things. LLOBI.com is my way of proving that even a Primary 5 student can create real, working technology.

🎯

Why I'm Building This

After PSLE, I hope to earn a DSA (Direct School Admission) place at a secondary school based on my technology background. LLOBI.com is my portfolio — a real, live website that shows what I can design, build, and ship.

Every game on this site represents something I learned: new code, new ideas, new problems I had to solve. I'm not just playing with technology — I'm building with it.
